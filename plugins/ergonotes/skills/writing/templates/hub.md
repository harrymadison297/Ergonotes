# Template: a hub (map of notes)

Title: the subject ("Spaced repetition"). Use it when five or more notes share a subject.

```markdown
A paragraph saying what this subject is and how the notes fit together.

## Core ideas
- [Note title](path.md): why it matters, in one line

## Practice
- [Note title](path.md): one line

## Sources
- [Note title](path.md): one line

## Open questions
- What is still unclear
```
Build it from `search_notes` and `get_links`; describe each link, do not just list.
